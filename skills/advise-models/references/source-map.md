# Source Map

| Claim | Sources | Confidence | Scope and caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choose models with task-specific, multi-metric evaluation rather than one leaderboard number. | [S-001](https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/), [S-002](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models) | High | HELM is comparative evidence across scenarios; provider docs describe only their own products. |
| Cascade/routing strategies can cut cost substantially while preserving quality on some workloads. | [S-003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05176) (FrugalGPT) | Medium | Reported savings are workload-specific; gains depend on router quality and validation cost. |
| Model capabilities, prices, and aliases are version-sensitive and change frequently. | [S-002](https://platform.openai.com/docs/models) | High | Re-check provider documentation at decision time; do not rely on numbers cached in advice. |
| Reasoning models suit hard, ambiguous, or accuracy-critical tasks; standard models suit speed- and cost-sensitive well-defined tasks. | [S-004](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices) | High | Model-family behavior evolves; validate the reasoning-vs-standard split on your own eval, not on provider positioning. |
| Accuracy varied by up to 15% across runs of settings meant to be deterministic, so repeated runs and variance estimates are needed when comparing models. | [S-005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04667) (Non-Determinism of "Deterministic" LLM Settings) | Medium | Preprint; five LLMs, eight tasks. |
| The same hosted service changed substantially within months (for example, prime identification fell from 84% to 51%), so version pinning and scheduled re-evaluation are needed. | [S-006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09009) (How is ChatGPT's behavior changing over time?) | Medium | Preprint; March versus June 2023 releases. |
| Routing on the model's own self-reflection (answer from retrieved context or escalate to long context) cut cost by 39% to 65% at comparable performance, so a self-report trigger can work but needs validation. | [S-007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16833) (Self-Route, EMNLP 2024 industry track) | High | Three 2024 models; validate any self-report trigger on held-out data. |
| Treat evaluations as experiments: standard errors and paired differences rather than bare averages. | [S-008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640) (Adding Error Bars to Evals) | High | Method paper. |
